      How it works

      Borrow the fungus's architecture, not its morality.

      In a fungal networkIn the System

      1. Hyphal tips sense and respond locally.

        Every satellite carries the same small trained policy, which senses risk and decides on its own.

        See it in the AI brain →

      2. One connected network.

        Satellites broadcast their intent to neighbours over “hyphae” links: shared data plus a handshake.

        See the handshake →

      3. A market with sanctions: partners that stop trading back get cut off (Kiers et al. 2011).

        A fairness ledger rewards those who give way and penalises free-riders.

        See the ledger →

      4. What nature lacks.

        Shared right-of-way rules, human escalation, allocated responsibility, governed learning and obligations to the future.

        See where the analogy breaks →
